Post-Presentation Q&A — Multiple-Choice Questions with Scripted Feedback
═══════════════════════════════════════
OVERVIEW
═══════════════════════════════════════
Format: 10 multiple-choice questions presented to every student after manuscript submission.
Question types:
- Single-answer (SA): One best answer among 4–5 options (7 questions)
- Select-all-that-apply (SATA): Student selects all correct options (1 question)
- K-type: Classic "I, II, III" combination format (2 questions)
This section is UNSCORED. No points contribute to the student's grade. The AI preceptor delivers scripted formative feedback for every possible answer selection.
Feedback structure per response:
- Correct/Incorrect statement
- What was strong (1 sentence)
- What was missed (1–2 sentences)
- Clinical pearl (1 sentence)

A. Start resmetirom 80 mg/day; reduce rosuvastatin to 20 mg/day
B. Start resmetirom 60 mg/day; reduce rosuvastatin to 20 mg/day
C. Start resmetirom 60 mg/day; discontinue rosuvastatin entirely
D. Start resmetirom 80 mg/day; no other changes needed
E. Delay resmetirom until clopidogrel is discontinued

At 82 kg, this patient should receive 60 mg/day, not the standard 80 mg. Missing this interaction could result in a 1.7-fold increase in resmetirom AUC.
