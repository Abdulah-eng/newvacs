Week 1 Hypertension Guideline Teaching Summary
Core Treatment Goal
For most adults with hypertension:
BP Goal: <130/80 mmHg
The 2025 AHA/ACC guideline emphasizes a universal treatment target of <130/80 mmHg for most adult patients.
Blood Pressure Classification
Normal:
<120/<80 mmHg
Elevated:
120–129 and <80 mmHg
Stage 1 Hypertension:
130–139 systolic OR 80–89 diastolic
Stage 2 Hypertension:
≥140 systolic OR ≥90 diastolic
Initial Evaluation
Students should assess:
Confirm accurate BP measurement
Review home BP readings when available
Assess ASCVD risk
Evaluate for diabetes
Evaluate kidney disease
Assess lifestyle contributors
Review medications that increase BP
Baseline labs:
BMP
SCr/eGFR
Potassium
Lipid panel
A1c or fasting glucose
UACR when appropriate
First-Line Drug Classes
Preferred agents:
ACE inhibitors
ARBs
Thiazide-type diuretics
Dihydropyridine calcium channel blockers
Monotherapy vs Combination Therapy
Monotherapy:
Stage 1 hypertension
Combination Therapy:
Stage 2 hypertension
BP >20/10 mmHg above goal
Single-pill combinations should be preferred when possible.
Follow-Up
Lifestyle only:
3–6 months
Medication initiation or adjustment:
Approximately 1 month
Lifestyle Counseling
Highest-yield interventions:
Weight reduction
DASH diet
Sodium restriction
Increased physical activity
Reduced alcohol intake
Tobacco cessation
Monitoring
Monitor:
BP response
Renal function
Potassium
Adverse effects
Adherence
Pharmacist-Specific Opportunities
Medication optimization
Home BP monitoring education
Adherence assessment
Cost barrier identification
Medication reconciliation
Common Student Mistakes
Treating one elevated reading as definitive hypertension
Failing to review home BP readings
Not checking renal function after ACEI/ARB initiation
Ignoring adherence before intensifying therapy
Missing secondary causes of hypertension
Escalation/Referral Considerations
Resistant hypertension
Suspected secondary hypertension
Rapid renal decline
Hypertensive urgency/emergency
Persistent uncontrolled BP despite guideline-directed therapy
