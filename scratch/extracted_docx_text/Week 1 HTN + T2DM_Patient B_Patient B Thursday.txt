Current antihypertensive therapy consists of lisinopril 20 mg daily and hydrochlorothiazide 25 mg daily, both of which are well tolerated.

PURPOSE: This is the single, authoritative rubric for the Patient B (James Wilson) Thursday Second 3-Month (6-Month) Follow-Up Ambulatory Care SOAP Note. Every subsection’s discrete input point values sum exactly to the subsection header, and all subsection headers sum exactly to 100.0. The model answer scores 100.0/100.0 against this rubric.
SCORING RULES:
•  FULL: Input present, accurate, and clinically contextualized (not merely a keyword).
•  HALF: Partially present — directionally correct but imprecise, or present without clinical context. Half-credit values are rounded to the nearest 0.05.
•  ZERO: Absent, or a factually incorrect statement is made about that input.
•  Additivity: Points are additive within each subsection → subsections additive within sections → sections additive to 100.0.
Acceptable-alternative inputs (e.g., therapeutic choices) list every guideline-supported answer in parentheses; any listed answer earns full credit.
