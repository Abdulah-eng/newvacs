Week 1 Type 2 Diabetes Teaching Summary
Glycemic Goals
General Goal:
A1c <7%
Goals should be individualized based on:
Age
Comorbidities
Life expectancy
Hypoglycemia risk
Patient preferences
Initial Evaluation
Assess:
A1c
eGFR
UACR
Weight/BMI
ASCVD history
Heart failure history
CKD status
First-Line Therapy
Most patients:
Metformin + lifestyle modification
Exceptions occur when:
ASCVD predominates
CKD predominates
Heart failure predominates
Significant obesity is present

Benefits:
Weight loss
A1c reduction
ASCVD benefit
Examples:
Semaglutide
Tirzepatide
Dulaglutide
SGLT2 Inhibitors
Benefits:
Kidney protection
Heart failure benefit
Cardiovascular benefit
Examples:
Empagliflozin
Dapagliflozin
Cardiorenal Protection
Presence of CKD, ASCVD, or HF should influence therapy selection independent of A1c goals.
Statin Therapy
Most adults age 40–75 years with diabetes should receive statin therapy.
