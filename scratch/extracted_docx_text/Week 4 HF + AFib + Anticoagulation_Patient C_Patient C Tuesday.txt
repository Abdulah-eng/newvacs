The patient demonstrates progressive CKD with eGFR declining from approximately 52 to 38 mL/min/1.73m² over the past year. While this trend warrants ongoing evaluation and monitoring, interpretation must occur within the broader clinical context.

Progressive CKD is common among patients with longstanding heart failure, diabetes, hypertension, and vascular disease.
Current renal function remains within ranges where sacubitril/valsartan, spironolactone, and empagliflozin may continue to be utilized with appropriate monitoring. The observed decline alone does not mandate discontinuation of therapy. Instead, careful longitudinal monitoring and reassessment of risk-benefit balance remain appropriate.
Patient education is particularly important given his concerns regarding dialysis and medication safety.
