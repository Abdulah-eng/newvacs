Question 1

Question ID: HLD-001

Disease State: Hyperlipidemia / ASCVD Risk Reduction

Topic: General lipid management goal

Difficulty: Foundational

Question Type: Single-best-answer

Question Stem:

A 58-year-old patient asks why his pharmacist is concerned about his cholesterol even though he feels well. Which response best reflects the overall goal of hyperlipidemia management?

Answer Choices:

A. To normalize all lipid values as quickly as possible

B. To reduce ASCVD risk through evidence-based LDL-C lowering and lifestyle modification

C. To treat only patients who already have symptoms of cardiovascular disease

D. To avoid statin therapy unless triglycerides are severely elevated

Correct Answer:

B. To reduce ASCVD risk through evidence-based LDL-C lowering and lifestyle modification

Rationale:

The primary goal of hyperlipidemia management is to reduce ASCVD risk through evidence-based LDL-C lowering and lifestyle modification. Patients may feel well but still have significant long-term cardiovascular risk.

Why the Incorrect Options Are Wrong:

A. The goal is not simply to normalize every lipid value as quickly as possible; treatment should be individualized based on ASCVD risk, LDL-C level, CKD status, diabetes status, medication tolerance, and patient preferences.

C. Hyperlipidemia management is preventive and should not wait until symptoms occur.

D. Statins are foundational for ASCVD risk reduction and are not limited to patients with severe hypertriglyceridemia.

Source Support:

2026 ACC/AHA Dyslipidemia Guideline; Week 2 Hyperlipidemia & ASCVD Risk Reduction Teaching Summary

Clinical Skill Tested:

Explaining the purpose of lipid-lowering therapy as cardiovascular risk reduction rather than symptom treatment.

QA Check:

Pass. The correct answer is clearly supported and the distractors reflect common misunderstandings about lipid management.
