Question ID: HTN-001
Disease State: Hypertension
Topic: Blood pressure classification
Difficulty: Foundational
Question Type: Single-best-answer
Question Stem:
A 48-year-old patient has repeated office blood pressure readings averaging 136/84 mmHg. Based on the 2025 AHA/ACC blood pressure classification framework, how should this blood pressure be classified?
Answer Choices:
A. Normal blood pressure
B. Elevated blood pressure
C. Stage 1 hypertension
D. Stage 2 hypertension
Correct Answer:
C. Stage 1 hypertension
Rationale:
Stage 1 hypertension is defined as systolic blood pressure 130–139 mmHg or diastolic blood pressure 80–89 mmHg. This patient’s average blood pressure of 136/84 mmHg meets both systolic and diastolic criteria for stage 1 hypertension.
Why the Incorrect Options Are Wrong:
A. Normal blood pressure is <120/<80 mmHg.
B. Elevated blood pressure is 120–129 systolic and <80 diastolic.
D. Stage 2 hypertension is ≥140 systolic or ≥90 diastolic.
Source Support:
2025 AHA/ACC Hypertension Guideline; HTN Guideline Teaching Summary
Clinical Skill Tested:
Classifying blood pressure correctly before making treatment decisions.
QA Check:
Pass. The correct answer is clearly supported and distractors reflect adjacent BP categories.
