Disease State: DepressionTopic: MDD diagnostic featuresDifficulty: FoundationalQuestion Type: Single-best-answer

Question Stem:According to the depression guideline framework, which symptom pattern best supports a diagnosis of major depressive disorder?

Answer Choices:A. One day of sadness after a stressful event without functional impairmentB. Depressed mood or loss of interest/pleasure with additional depressive symptoms for at least 2 weeks causing clinically significant impairmentC. Excessive worry for 6 months without depressive symptomsD. Nicotine cravings that occur only after meals

Correct Answer:B. Depressed mood or loss of interest/pleasure with additional depressive symptoms for at least 2 weeks causing clinically significant impairment
