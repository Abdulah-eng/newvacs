Week 6 Assignment: New Drug Review —  Resmetirom  ( Rezdiffra ®) in MASH Virtual Ambulatory Care Simulation (VACS) Week 6 — Capstone Assignment --- Assignment Overview This capstone assignment is a grand rounds-style new drug review on  resmetirom  ( Rezdiffra ®), the first FDA-approved pharmacotherapy for metabolic dysfunction-associated steatohepatitis (MASH). The final deliverable is a written manuscript formatted as a comprehensive drug monograph suitable for presentation at a  pharmacy department grand rounds  — the type of continuing education presentation delivered to pharmacy students, pharmacists, and faculty. This assignment will be the sole focus of Week 6 (the final week of the rotation). Upon submission, preceptor feedback will be provided based on the completeness, accuracy, and clinical reasoning demonstrated in the manuscript. --- Deliverable One Microsoft Word  document (. docx) containing the  complete  new drug review manuscript. Upload the final document to the VACS site by the end of Week 6. --- Formatting Requirements - Font: Arial, 11-point - Spacing: Single-spaced - Margins: 1 inch on all sides - Citation style: AMA (American Medical Association) format — numbered superscript citations in the order they appear in the text - A references page must be included at the end of the document - No page limit — the manuscript should be as thorough as needed to cover all required sections at a depth appropriate for a 45- to 60-minute grand rounds presentation - Tables and figures may be included where they enhance clarity (e.g., trial results summary tables, comparison charts, monitoring parameter tables) --- Audience Write this manuscript as though it will be presented at a  pharmacy department grand rounds  to an audience of pharmacy students, clinical pharmacists, and pharmacy faculty. The tone should be professional, evidence-based, and educational —  similar to  a continuing education presentation. Assume the audience has a general pharmacy knowledge base but no specialized expertise in hepatology or MASH. --- Required Articles The following three articles must be incorporated into the manuscript. Each article should be meaningfully discussed — not merely cited in passing. 1.  MAESTRO-NASH Trial (Pivotal Efficacy Trial)    Harrison SA,  Bedossa  P, Guy CD, et al. A Phase 3, Randomized, Controlled Trial of  Resmetirom  in NASH with Liver Fibrosis.  N Engl J Med.  2024;390(6):497-509. Link: https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2309000 2.  MAESTRO-NAFLD-1 Trial (Safety and Biomarker Trial)    Harrison SA, Taub R, Neff GW, et al.  Resmetirom  for Nonalcoholic Fatty Liver Disease: A Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Phase 3 Trial.  Nat Med.  2023;29(11):2919-2928. Link: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-023-02603-1 3.  AASLD Practice Guidance (Clinical Implementation)    Chen VL, Morgan TR, Rotman Y, et al.  Resmetirom  Therapy for Metabolic Dysfunction-Associated  Steatotic  Liver Disease: October 2024 Updates to AASLD Practice Guidance.  Hepatology.  2025;81(5):1774-1786.    Link: https://journals.lww.com/hep/fulltext/2025/05000/resmetirom therapy for metabolic.30.aspx Additional sources beyond these three (e.g., the FDA-approved prescribing label, review articles, UpToDate, other primary literature) are encouraged but not required. --- Required Sections The manuscript must include  all of  the following sections. A brief description of expectations for each section is provided below. 1. Disease State Background Provide an overview of MASLD/MASH including current nomenclature, epidemiology, disease burden, natural history and progression (fibrosis, cirrhosis, hepatocellular carcinoma), pathophysiology, and the treatment landscape prior to  resmetirom's  approval. Establish the unmet clinical need that  resmetirom  addresses. Connect the underlying disease biology to the drug's mechanism of action. 2. Pharmacology Describe  resmetirom's  mechanism of action, including thyroid hormone receptor selectivity and downstream metabolic effects. Cover clinically relevant pharmacokinetics (absorption, metabolism, distribution). Provide a thorough discussion of drug interactions, including specific medications that are contraindicated, require dose adjustments, or have dose caps when used concomitantly with  resmetirom . 3. Pivotal Efficacy Trial — MAESTRO-NASH Provide a detailed analysis of the MAESTRO-NASH trial (Harrison et al.,  NEJM  2024). This section should include: - Study design and methods (phase, randomization, blinding, population, inclusion/exclusion criteria) - Primary and key secondary endpoints with definitions - Efficacy results with specific data - A critical appraisal discussing the strengths and limitations of the trial, including the regulatory context (accelerated approval, surrogate endpoints), generalizability, and any notable features of the results This is the most important section of the manuscript. A strong critical appraisal goes beyond listing limitations — it interprets how each limitation affects the clinical interpretation of the results. 4. Safety Trial — MAESTRO-NAFLD-1 Provide a detailed analysis of the MAESTRO-NAFLD-1 trial (Harrison et al.,  Nat Med  2023). This section should include: - Study design and how it differs from MAESTRO-NASH (population, endpoints, purpose) - Safety results including common adverse events, serious adverse events, and notable safety signals - Key secondary efficacy endpoints (imaging and lipid biomarkers) and how they complement the MAESTRO-NASH biopsy data 5. Clinical Implementation — AASLD Practice Guidance Summarize the AASLD Practice Guidance (Chen et al.,  Hepatology  2025) as it applies to  resmetirom  use in clinical practice. This section should include: - Patient selection and screening ( noninvasive  testing pathways and thresholds) - Dosing recommendations and dose adjustments - Monitoring parameters and schedule - Contraindications and special populations 6. Place in Therapy and Formulary Recommendation Position  resmetirom  relative to existing management strategies (lifestyle modification, off-label pharmacotherapy). Discuss cost and access considerations. Provide a clear, evidence-based formulary recommendation with specific criteria for use, as though presenting to a pharmacy and therapeutics (P&amp;T) committee. 7. Patient Counseling and Ambulatory Care Pharmacy Application Outline key patient counseling points a pharmacist should cover when initiating  resmetirom . Describe the role of the ambulatory care pharmacist in MASH management —  from screening and identification of eligible patients,  to medication reconciliation, monitoring, and collaborative care. --- Tips for  a Strong  Submission -  Lead with clinical relevance.  A grand rounds audience wants to know why this drug matters before diving into the data. Frame the disease state and unmet need in a way that captures attention. -  Be specific with data.  Report exact efficacy and safety numbers from the trials. Approximate or vague descriptions of results (e.g., "the drug was effective") do not demonstrate mastery of the evidence. -  Critical appraisal is what separates good from great.  Do not simply list study limitations. Explain how each limitation affects the interpretation of the results and what it means for clinical decision-making. -  Make it actionable.  The audience should walk away knowing exactly which patients are eligible,  what dose  to use, what to monitor, what drug interactions to watch for, and what to tell the patient. Tables summarizing these practical details are highly effective. -  Connect the three required articles.  The strongest manuscripts weave the efficacy data (MAESTRO-NASH), safety data (MAESTRO-NAFLD-1), and clinical guidance (AASLD) into a cohesive narrative rather than treating them as isolated summaries. -  Use AMA citation format consistently.  Number references in the  order they  first appear in the text using superscript numerals. The references page should list all sources in numerical order with complete bibliographic information. --- Submission Instructions Upload the completed Word  document (. docx) to the VACS site before the end of Week 6. Preceptor feedback will be provided after submission, including section-by-section commentary on strengths, areas for improvement, and any corrections. --- Good luck — this is the capstone of your VACS rotation. Approach it as you would a real grand rounds presentation to your pharmacy colleagues.
